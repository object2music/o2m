One recording under several Spotify uris (an album and a compilation, a remaster…): same title, same main artist, same length, at least one of them played. Merging adds the plays of the others to the one kept, moves their play log, and counts their future plays on it. Undo stays possible. Editing must be unlocked on the Mood page. Versions sharing one ISRC (Spotify's recording code) are merged without asking, at every start or with the button below; what is listed here is what that cannot settle.
